# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — first public release

- Skill workflow A–G: discover, applicability, gaps, plan, implement, verify, report.
- Requirement catalog: 40 IDs (5 scope gates and 35 obligations) mapped to the DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025.
- Validator that enforces citation discipline, truthful test records, approval gates, and no compliance guarantees.
- Two worked examples: one out of scope (`pocket-ledger`) and one in scope (`campus-clinic`, three rounds of independent verification).
- Benchmark v0 (12 cases), deterministic scorer, and pilot results comparing the skill with no skill (`benchmark/RESULTS.md`).
